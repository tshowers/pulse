Pulse Help

Questions · Responses · Signal · Action

Every response is a signal.

Pulse is a quick way to ask the people you serve a few focused questions and turn their answers into your next decision. Build a short survey, share one link, read the results, and repeat. Over time, Pulse Home shows whether you are really listening.

Building Pulses is free. Publishing and collecting responses requires a Pulse plan.

Who Pulse is for

Anyone who makes decisions about people they serve

If you have customers, users, or a team and you usually decide by guessing, Pulse replaces the guess with a quick answer from the people it affects.

What that looks like

Before

A bakery owner thinks lunch sales are slow because of prices and plans a discount.

The Pulse

Three questions on the receipt link: “What brings you in?”, “What would make you come at lunch?”, and one open comment.

After

Most answers say lunch is too slow for a work break, not too pricey. They add a grab-and-go shelf instead of cutting prices.

An illustrative example. The point: one short Pulse can change which problem you solve.

Meet TODD

TODD is your assistant inside Pulse.

On Pulse Home, TODD reads your Pulses and responses, flags where your feedback loop is stuck, and shows what it is tracking for you.

On any page, move your mouse to the bottom-right corner and click Talk to TODD. Ask about the Pulse you are building, the page you are on, or what to do next. TODD can see which page you are on, so you don’t have to explain.

On a phone or tablet, tap the bottom-right area to show the button.

Getting the most out of Pulse

Run it as a habit, not a one-off

The people who get the most from Pulse ask small questions often. Each Pulse answers one decision, and the next Pulse checks whether the change worked.

  1. DecidePick one decision you are unsure about.
  2. AskWrite a 3–5 question Pulse and publish it.
  3. ShareSend the link to one specific group.
  4. ReadReview results within a few days.
  5. ActMake one change and tell people you did.
