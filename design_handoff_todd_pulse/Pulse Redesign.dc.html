TODD Pulse redesign{{ themeLabel }}
Pulse in the Find look. Every pulse now moves through the same three steps, shown at the top of each screen: Write, Share, Results. Each screen has one main button, so it's always clear what to do next. TODD writes the questions if you want, reviews them while you build, and turns the answers into a summary with a next move. The seven buttons on the old command view, the separate dashboard and the Customer Health board fold into these screens.
Round 2: public pages, answering on a computer, and iOSLanding, About and Help (2a–2c) for people who haven't signed up yet, written around Write, Share, Results. What a recipient sees on a computer after opening the link (2d–2e). Then the whole app on iPhone (2f–2n). The Customer Health meters and Care Cycle explainer are gone from Help, since that board is gone.
